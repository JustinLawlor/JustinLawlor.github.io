Consul

  • Wrote the chapter's 2021–22 strategic plan, with goals and owners for philanthropy, recruitment, alumni relations, academics and officer support.

Recruitment Chair

  • Kept every event within public health rules, including social distancing and provincial gathering limits.
  • Held active members accountable for meeting each potential new member, so every recruit was introduced to the whole chapter.
  • Recruited 20 new members, and produced rush cards and a recruitment video (Photoshop, Premiere Pro).
