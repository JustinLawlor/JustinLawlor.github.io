Sigma Chi, Beta Omega Chapter

Consul (chapter president), 2021 to 2022 · Recruitment Chair, 2020 to 2021

I joined Sigma Chi in my first year and lived in the chapter house. I held two elected roles, both during COVID, when most of what the chapter did had to be rebuilt to run online and then moved back in person.

Consul

  • Wrote the chapter's 2021–22 strategic plan, with goals and owners for philanthropy, recruitment, alumni relations, academics and officer support.
  • Ran weekly chapter meetings and led an executive committee of 13 officers, including end-of-term officer evaluations.
  • Recruited 30 new members during the year.
  • Proposed and passed bylaw changes that defined mandatory chapter activities and added a lighter first step for accountability before a formal hearing.
  • Started planning for the chapter's 100th anniversary in 2022 with alumni.
  • Kept relationships with other fraternities and sororities on campus, and acted as a neutral voice when disputes came up in the house.
  • Wrote a transition plan for the next Consul.

Recruitment Chair

  • Moved recruitment online when COVID closed campus. I set funnel targets, ran Instagram outreach to incoming students, tracked every prospect in a shared sheet and held virtual speed interviews.
  • Recruited 20 new members.
  • Wrote the recruitment strategy and produced rush cards and a recruitment video (Photoshop, Premiere Pro).
  • Wrote a recruitment playbook for my successor covering events, marketing, attendance rules and how to run bid session.

Philanthropy

Our main fundraiser was Derby Days. The chapter raised $10,000 for the Huntsman Cancer Institute in one year and about $5,000 in another.
