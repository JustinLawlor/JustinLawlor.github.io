Giving

I try to do as much good as I can with my time and money, following the ideas of effective altruism. I give every month through RC Forward, a Canadian platform for donating to high-impact charities, and I donate plasma once a week.

Volunteering

  • Toronto Waterfront Marathon: course marshal at the 2026 race, keeping runners on route and the course clear.
  • Alzheimer Society of York Region: volunteered at their donor appreciation event in Aurora in October 2026, setting up, welcoming guests at the door and coat check, and helping with teardown.

Interests

  • Running and strength training. I ran the 2024 Toronto Waterfront Marathon. These days I run for my health and lift a few mornings a week.
  • Philosophy. I took philosophy electives at TMU and kept reading after. Camus's The Myth of Sisyphus stuck with me, and I listen to podcasts on Socrates, Aristotle and Descartes. Lately I've been most interested in metaethics.
  • Anime. Some favourites: Monster, Steins;Gate, Death Note, Attack on Titan, Re:Zero, Megalobox and Gachiakuta.
  • Music. I produced beats through high school and university. Now I mostly listen and explore: metal subgenres like death and progressive metal, EDM and indie are always in rotation, and I branch out into things like jazz.
